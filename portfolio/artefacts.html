Seven projects exploring illustration, composition, identity and typography.

01

House with Lines

Original artefact

Software: Microsoft Paint

The Line and Shape tools define the roof, walls, windows and door, while Fill with Color creates the solid areas. Bold outlines, repeated window shapes and contrasting colours demonstrate geometric construction, repetition and visual emphasis.

Interactive PowerPoint

Preview to be added

Software: Microsoft PowerPoint

Slide layouts and text boxes organise the content, while hyperlinks and action buttons support navigation. This demonstrates visual hierarchy, consistent presentation design and interactive communication.

03

Basket of Fruits

Original artefact

Software: Photopea

Photopea's selection, masking and transform tools support this layered fruit-basket scene. The composition demonstrates image compositing, proportion, overlap and depth, while the bright fruit contrasts with the dark patterned background.

04

Girl and House

Original artefact

Software: Photopea

Selection and masking tools blend the girl, house, sky and foreground plants across layers. Resizing, placement and colour adjustments create a unified scene, demonstrating image compositing, proportion, depth and an atmospheric mood.
